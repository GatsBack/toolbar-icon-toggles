# Toolbar Icon Toggles for Lumiverse

**Toolbar Icon Toggles** is a Lumiverse extension that lets you clean up your interface by toggling the visibility of any icon or button in the app. Pick any element on your screen, add it to your personal list, and hide or show it whenever you want.

---

## Features

* 🎯 **Visual Element Picker:** Click "+ Add icon" and select any button or toolbar item directly on the page to start managing it.
* 👁️ **Instant Visibility Toggles:** Hide or show individual toolbar icons with a single click.
* ⚡ **Toggle All Switch:** Quickly hide or restore all managed icons at once.
* 🔍 **Search & Filter:** Easily locate specific toggles in your list as your saved collection grows.
* ✏️ **Rename & Reorder:** Custom-label your icons and use the **▲ / ▼** buttons to arrange them in the order you prefer.
* 💾 **Persistent Settings:** Your hidden preferences are saved to your account and persist automatically across page refreshes.
